Nuée for Android

Download Nuée
  1. Open the downloaded file, and allow your browser to install apps if Android asks.
  2. Open Nuée, and enter your nuée with your mail address, or with the code one of your devices shows.

Nuée for Linux

In a terminal:

curl -fsSL https://aco-hub.github.io/nuee-releases/install.sh | sh
  1. It installs Nuée the way your distribution expects: Debian, Ubuntu, Fedora, openSUSE, Arch and the others.
  2. Open “Nuée” from your applications menu.
